Narayan Surve (1926–2010) was a Marathi-language poet from Mumbai, widely regarded as one of the foremost voices of the Dalit literary movement. He was raised in an orphanage, worked as a mill laborer, and came to poetry through the labor movement. His work is stark, plainspoken, and quietly devastating.

Translating Surve is a problem of register. His Marathi is deliberately simple — he is writing for and about people who did not have access to literary culture — but in English, that simplicity risks reading as naivety. I have tried to keep his directness without flattening it.

The following translations are my own. Any errors of interpretation are mine.

---

*I have accepted this, and so I continue —*
*but will the others of our time?*

*I feel the gentle press of the seed*
*through the soil of our time.*
